  • OEM lead: Smartech Technology, model SP-1812

Serial line specs

  • 9600 baud · 8 data bits · no parity · 1 stop bit
  • Observed: five-byte groups, puck and pen
  • Example: 99 18 11 1d 0c · hex · synthetic example from grid E median
  • Coordinate hypothesis: bytes 0–4; u = b1 + 128 × b2, v = b3 + 128 × b4
  • 7-bit pairs: low byte + 128 × high byte

    Axes: u rightward · v upward
